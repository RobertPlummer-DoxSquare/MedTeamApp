MedTeam Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

MedTeam (“we”, “us”) is a professional networking app for clinicians and medical students. It is built for National Medical Association (NMA) members to find mentors, collaborators, and referral partners. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, username, and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication, and we never see them.
  • Professional profile: your credentials, degree, specialty, subspecialties, board certifications, training history, institution, practice type, state licenses, languages, NMA region, and your availability (mentoring, collaboration, referrals).
  • Verification: clinicians give their NPI number, and students give their school email address.
  • Contact details you choose to share: for example, an office phone number for referrals.
  • Messages: the requests, messages, and research proposals you send to other members.
  • Safety reports: your reports about other members, and the list of members you have blocked.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your profile in the member directory, deliver your messages, and keep you signed in.
  • To verify members. We check NPI numbers against the public NPPES registry run by the U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services.
  • To keep the community safe. We review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, and remove members who break them.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in members can see your profile. The app does not show them your email, NPI number, or school email. Your office phone is shown only if you turn on referrals.
  • Your messages can be seen only by the people in that conversation. We may also review them when someone reports the conversation.
  • Service providers: we store data with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it for us.
  • The law: we may disclose information when the law requires it.

No patient information

MedTeam is not meant for protected health information. Please do not share patient names or other identifying patient details in messages or on your profile.

Blocking and reporting

You can block or report any member from their profile or from a conversation. A member you block can no longer find you in the directory or message you, and your conversations with them are closed. They are not told that you blocked them. We review reports within 24 hours.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. To delete your account, go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently removes your profile and login. Messages you already sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, with your name shown as “Deleted user”.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

MedTeam is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update here and change the effective date above.
