MedTeam Support

Help for MedTeam on iPhone

MedTeam is a professional networking app for clinicians and medical students. It helps National Medical Association (NMA) members find mentors, research collaborators, and referral partners. If you have a question or a problem, or want to send feedback, we’re here to help.

Contact us

Email doxsquares@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

To help us solve the problem faster, please include:

  • The email address on your MedTeam account
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • What happened and what you expected to happen, with screenshots if you can

Please do not include patient information in support emails.

Frequently asked questions

How does verification work?

Clinicians are verified by NPI number, which we check against the public NPPES registry. Medical students are verified with their school email address. Verified members show a “Verified clinician” badge on their profile.

How do I request mentorship or propose a research collaboration?

Find the member in the Directory, open their profile, and tap Connect. Your request appears in their Inbox. Once they accept it, you can message each other.

How do referrals work?

Referrals now happen by phone. Open a member’s profile and tap Call office for referrals. This is only available for members who have turned on referrals.

How do I block or report someone?

Tap the ••• menu on the member’s profile or in your conversation with them, then choose Block or Report. A member you block can no longer find you or message you, and they are not told that you blocked them. We review reports within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently removes your profile and login. Messages you already sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, with your name shown as “Deleted user”.

Can I share patient information?

No. MedTeam is not meant for protected health information. Please do not share patient names or other identifying patient details.
